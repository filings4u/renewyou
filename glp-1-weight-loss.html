What GLP-1 care means

More than a prescription

GLP-1–based medications are prescription treatments that affect pathways involved in appetite, digestion, and blood sugar regulation. Medication is only one part of care. We evaluate your health history, goals, current medications, potential risks, and overall treatment plan before deciding whether a GLP-1–based option is medically appropriate.

Eligibility

Is GLP-1 treatment right for you?

Eligibility cannot be determined from a website alone. A clinician needs to review your individual health information first.

  • Your current weight, health goals, and weight-related risk factors
  • Your medical and surgical history
  • Current prescriptions, supplements, and medication interactions
  • Previous weight-management approaches and response
  • Potential contraindications, side effects, and safety concerns
  • Whether laboratory testing or additional evaluation is appropriate
Treatment options

Individualized medication decisions

Depending on your evaluation, a provider may discuss GLP-1–based prescription options such as semaglutide or other medications in this treatment category. Some newer medicines act on more than one metabolic pathway. The specific medication, if any, depends on your clinical needs, eligibility, availability, and provider judgment.

Important: GLP-1 medications are prescription treatments and are not appropriate for everyone. Results vary, and no specific amount of weight loss is guaranteed. Your provider will review risks, benefits, side effects, and alternatives before prescribing.
Frequently asked questions

GLP-1 weight loss FAQs

What are GLP-1 medications?

GLP-1–based prescription medications affect pathways involved in appetite, digestion, and blood sugar regulation. A medical provider determines whether treatment is appropriate based on your health history, current medications, goals, and clinical evaluation.

Who may be eligible for GLP-1 weight loss treatment?

Eligibility is individualized. Your provider reviews factors such as weight-related health risks, medical history, medications, prior weight-management efforts, and potential contraindications before recommending treatment.

Do I need follow-up visits?

Yes. Follow-up helps your provider monitor progress, side effects, nutrition, medication response, and whether treatment should be adjusted or continued.

How quickly will I lose weight?

There is no guaranteed timeline or amount of weight loss. Response varies between patients and depends on many factors, including the treatment plan, medication response, nutrition, activity, health conditions, and follow-up.

Can I schedule a consultation even if I am not sure I qualify?

Yes. An initial consultation is the appropriate place to discuss your goals and determine whether GLP-1–based treatment or another medical weight-management approach may be suitable for you.
