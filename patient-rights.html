Patient Rights Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

At ReNew You Health & Wellness, we are committed to ensuring that our patients are treated with respect and dignity. Our Patient Rights Policy outlines the rights of our patients to promote a supportive and positive healthcare experience.

Patient Rights

1. Right to Respect and Dignity

Patients have the right to be treated with respect, dignity, and compassion by all healthcare providers.

2. Right to Information

Patients have the right to receive complete and understandable information about their diagnosis, treatment options, and prognosis in a language they can understand.

3. Right to Privacy

Patients have the right to confidentiality of their personal health information and to participate in decisions about their treatment.

4. Right to Participate in Care Decisions

Patients have the right to be actively involved in decisions regarding their healthcare, including the right to refuse treatment.

5. Right to Access Medical Records

Patients have the right to access their medical records within a reasonable timeframe and request amendments to their records if they believe the information is incorrect.

6. Right to Safety

Patients have the right to receive care in a safe environment free from abuse or neglect.

7. Right to File Complaints

Patients have the right to voice complaints and suggestions regarding their care without fear of retaliation.

8. Right to Timely Care

Patients have the right to receive timely evaluation and treatment.

9. Right to Support Persons

Patients have the right to have a support person of their choice present during discussions about their care and treatment, as well as during medical procedures when appropriate.

10. Right to Non-Discrimination

Patients have the right to be treated without discrimination based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, or sexual orientation.
