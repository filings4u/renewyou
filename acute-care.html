Acute Care for Non-Emergency Health Concerns in Chicago Heights

When an illness or minor health concern needs timely evaluation, ReNew You Health & Wellness offers acute care in Chicago Heights for appropriate non-emergency needs. Our goal is to provide clear guidance, an individualized evaluation, and a convenient next step for your care.

For ongoing wellness needs, you can also explore wellness services, IV hydration therapy, or contact the clinic to ask about availability. For life-threatening symptoms or emergencies, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
