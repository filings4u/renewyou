Good Faith Estimate Policy

Effective Date: August 2026   |   Last Updated: August 2026

Policy Overview

As a healthcare provider, ReNew You Health & Wellness is committed to transparency and open communication regarding the costs of services to our patients. In compliance with the No Surprises Act, we offer Good Faith Estimates (GFEs) to help patients understand their financial obligations for scheduled services.

What is a Good Faith Estimate?

A Good Faith Estimate is a written estimate of the expected charges for medical services based on the information available at the time. This estimate is provided prior to the delivery of services to ensure that patients are informed about their potential costs.

How to Request a Good Faith Estimate

  1. Contact Us: Patients can request a Good Faith Estimate by reaching out to us directly via phone or email.
  2. Information Required: Please provide your name, contact information, the specific services you are inquiring about, and any relevant insurance information.
  3. Review of Estimate: Once the request is received, we will review the details and provide an estimate based on the services requested.

Timeframe

Patients can expect to receive their Good Faith Estimate within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 1-3 business days.

Understanding Your Estimate

The Good Faith Estimate will include:

  • A description of the services to be provided.
  • Expected costs associated with each service.
  • Information regarding any potential additional services that may be required.

Changes in Estimate

Please note that this estimate is based on current information and may be subject to change. Factors such as changes in the patient’s condition, additional tests, or unanticipated procedures may alter the final cost.

Insurance Coverage

If you have health insurance, please verify your coverage directly with your insurer. The GFE is not a guarantee of insurance coverage or payment.
