Verifying Your Benefits

Understanding your insurance benefits can sometimes be complex. Coverage limits vary significantly depending on your specific plan or employer policy group tier. We recommend taking these simple steps prior to your scheduled clinic appointment:

1. Contact Your Provider

Call the toll-free number on the back of your insurance card or check their online member account portal tools to access immediate coverage files.

2. Inquire About Services

Ask about your specific plan coverage details for preventative clinical checkups, medical weight management, or diagnostic wellness offerings.

3. Contact Us Directly

Our team is here to help! Provide your card details via phone at 708-329-2155 or email info@renewyouhealthwellness.com and we will assist you in navigating coverage.
