ReNew You Health & Wellness SMS Policy Overview

At ReNew You Health & Wellness, we value your privacy and aim to provide a seamless communication experience. By providing your mobile number, you consent to receive appointment reminders via SMS.

1. SMS Services

  • We only send text messages for appointment reminders.
  • SMS communication is initiated only when you provide your phone number through the required form.

2. Opt-In Requirements

  • To receive SMS reminders, you must complete a form that requires your phone number.
  • Providing your number is entirely voluntary, and you are not required to provide it to receive services.

3. Message Frequency

You can expect to receive reminders and occasional updates. The frequency will vary depending on your appointment schedule.

4. Charges

Standard messaging and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

5. Opt-Out Information

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive.
